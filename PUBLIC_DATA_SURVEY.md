# Public PTM, kinase, interaction and IDR data: what can be integrated

A survey made on 27 October 2026 for the acidic-IDR work (`ACIDIC_IDR_PROPOSAL.md`) and the IDR Explorer
([Shechterlab/idr-explorer](https://github.com/Shechterlab/idr-explorer)): which public resources add charge-relevant
sites, kinase attribution, partner networks or IDR annotation, under what licence, and from which URL. Every DOI was
resolved against the Crossref API (or doi.org for DataCite DOIs) and its title matched.

## How to read the tables

- **Redistribution OK?** means: may a *derived per-site table* (UniProt accession, position, residue, evidence/score columns) be shown and downloadable on a public, non-commercial site whose data are released under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**?
  - **yes**: CC0, CC BY 4.0, MIT, CC BY-NC 4.0 (BY-NC material may be adapted into an NC-SA release), CC BY-NC-SA 3.0/4.0 (the 3.0 ShareAlike clause allows "a later version of this License with the same License Elements").
  - **no**: an explicit prohibition, or no licence plus aggregation of restricted sources.
  - **unclear**: no licence stated, CC BY-NC-ND (ND forbids sharing adapted material; whether extracted site coordinates are protected is a legal question, and EU database right may apply), or conflicting statements.
- **Reachable** is the HTTP status of a lightweight request (`curl -sS -I`, or `-r 0-1000`) on the download URL. 200 = HEAD OK; 206 = range request honoured. File sizes are from `Content-Length`.
- **UNVERIFIED** marks anything not confirmed from a primary source.
- Software licences (GPL, LGPL, MIT) govern the code, not numbers you compute with it.

## Notes and caveats

1. **Sequence versions.** PTMeXchange builds were searched against UniProt UP000005640 from 2024-04-04, and AlphaFold v6 is built on UniProt 2025_03. This repository uses the 2026_03 canonical sequences. Remap positions by sequence or peptide, not by trusting coordinates; drop sites whose residue does not match.
2. **The UniProt Proteins API is the licence-cleanest single route** to reprocessed human phospho (Ochoa PXD012174), acetyl, Arg/Lys-methyl, SUMO and ubiquitin sites with harmonised confidence. The PRIDE copies of the PTMeXchange human builds (PXD068489, PXD068989, PXD079218, PXD076372) and PXD012174 are **CC0** according to the PRIDE API `license` field.
3. **Hosts that were unreachable or broken on 27 October 2026:** SIGNOR (connection reset / 503), RegPhos (503 timeout), PhaSePro (timeout), ADPriboDB (expired TLS certificate), oglcnac.mcw.edu and data.glygen.org (TLS chain errors), bioRxiv (429), Europe PMC REST (intermittent 503 late in the session), and AlphaFold per-entry files (403).
4. **Security note:** the EPSD download page (epsd.biocuckoo.cn) contains injected gambling-spam links. Treat anything downloaded from it as untrusted.

## Ranked shortlist (most valuable integrations for this project)

1. **UniProt Proteins API `proteomics/ptm` (PTMeXchange human builds plus Ochoa PXD012174), CC BY 4.0 (underlying PRIDE copies CC0).** *Partly done: the PTMeXchange files are staged (`scripts/54`); the reprocessed phosphosites (PXD012174), which the API serves and no file does, are not.* This is one harmonised, residue-level, confidence-graded (Gold/Silver/Bronze, FLR) source covering every charge-modifying PTM tracked here except ADPr and citrullination: 1,771 Arg-methyl sites for basic segments, 17,700 acetyl-Lys, 115k ubiquitin-Lys, 38k SUMO and about 116k reprocessed phosphosites. It is fully redistributable.
2. **Johnson 2023 / Yaron-Barir 2024 PSSMs (Supplementary Table 2, CC BY 4.0), scored locally with `kinase-library` (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0).** These give CK2A1/CK2A2 and other acidophilic-kinase (CK1, GRK, PLK) scores and percentiles for every S/T in acidic segments, including sites nobody has observed. Do not ship the PSP-derived rows of Table 3.
3. **Koenig et al. 2026 phosphoproteome atlas (Zenodo, CC BY 4.0).** About 200k class I sites across 33 cell lines plus a **site-stoichiometry** table. It is the most modern deep phospho layer that can be redistributed, and the only open phospho stoichiometry found. Skip the PSP-derived Table 7.
4. **Rebak et al. 2024 citrullinome (CC BY 4.0; PRIDE CC0).** 14,056 sites, with an explicit overlap with Arg methylation (186 shared sites). This directly serves the "citrullination competes with methylation on basic segments" question.
5. **STRING v12.5 physical subnetwork (CC BY 4.0), cross-checked with HuRI (CC BY 4.0), IntAct (CC BY) and Complex Portal (CC0).** *Done for STRING, Complex Portal and IntAct (the trans test found no complementarity beyond chance); HuRI not yet.* These are the partner networks for the acidic×basic pairing test. HuRI gives binary-only edges, STRING gives scored physical links, and Complex Portal supplies complex context. BioGRID (MIT) is the fallback.
6. **Nielsen-lab ADP-ribosylomes: Hendriks 2021 Nat Commun (CC BY 4.0; 2,758 sites) plus Hendriks 2019 MCP (7,040 sites; Crossref says CC BY 4.0, PMC copy says exclusive licence, so confirm).** These are the only redistributable residue-level Ser-ADPr sets. Hendriks 2021 also documents hydroxylamine artefacts relevant to the Glu/Asp-ADPr claims (Zhen 2017, CC BY-NC-ND).
7. **OmniPath enzyme–substrate, filtered per record to compatible sources (mainly SIGNOR CC BY 4.0).** About 19.8k human records and 499 CK2 records with a redistributable source. This adds curated kinase attribution next to the PSSM predictions.
8. **sparrow (MIT) for κ/SCD plus FINCHES (CC BY-NC 4.0) for predicted IDR–IDR ε between acidic and basic segments, with DisProt 2026_06 (CC BY 4.0) as experimental disorder ground truth.** These give the charge-patterning metrics and a sequence-based prior for the pairing test. Hansen 2019 acetyl stoichiometry (CC BY 4.0, 6,829 sites) is the runner-up for this slot.

**Confirmed absent:** there is no proteome-scale human glutamylation site dataset. UniProt has 14 reviewed human entries with a polyglutamate MOD_RES, the PRIDE "glutamylation" search shows only PXD065251, and van Dijk 2008 is protein-level only.
