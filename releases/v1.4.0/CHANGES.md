### Xenograft-only sites

Ma et al. 2023 measured breast cancer patient-derived xenografts grown in mice. The 46 sites only they report carry the flag `xenograft_only`: where the human and mouse sequences agree, the peptide can come from the mouse stroma.

## New annotation layers

v1.4.0 adds 5 layers of annotation beside the sites. None adds, removes or moves a site; their columns, tables and tests are in `README.md`, `data_dictionary.tsv` and the statistics table, and scripts/release_layers.py builds them.

### Cancer cell lines and tumours

Cancer genetics beside the sites, not evidence about methylation: how much DepMap's CRISPR-screened cancer cell lines depend on each methylated protein (protein columns `depmap_*`), and the missense and stop changes of each methylarginine in TCGA tumours (the MC3 calls of the PanCancer Atlas, from the GDC) and DepMap cell lines (site columns `tcga_changes`, `depmap_changes`). Two tests: dependency classes of the methylated proteins (analysis `depmap_dependency`) and changes at methylarginines against the other arginines of their proteins (analysis `cancer_change`).

- 3,100 sites have a change in at least one tumour and 849 in at least one cell line.

Source: DepMap Public 24Q4 (doi:10.25452/figshare.plus.27993248; Tsherniak et al. 2017, doi:10.1016/j.cell.2017.06.010; Chronos, Dempster et al. 2021, doi:10.1186/s13059-021-02540-7); PRISM Repurposing 24Q2 (doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.25917643; Corsello et al. 2020, doi:10.1038/s43018-019-0018-6); the results shown here are in whole or part based upon data generated by the TCGA Research Network (https://www.cancer.gov/tcga): MC3 somatic calls (Ellrott et al. 2018, doi:10.1016/j.cels.2018.03.002), the calls of the PanCancer Atlas (Hoadley et al. 2018, doi:10.1016/j.cell.2018.03.022), from the GDC (mc3.v0.2.8.PUBLIC.maf.gz). Licence: DepMap Public 24Q4 and PRISM Repurposing 24Q2: CC BY 4.0; TCGA MC3 calls: GDC open-access data (NIH Genomic Data Sharing policy, no licence stated).

### Writer perturbations (PRMT knockouts, knockdowns and inhibitors)

Knockouts, knockdowns and inhibitors of the PRMTs that quantified methylated peptides. Each is called by the release's rule (a peptide changes at |log2| >= 1 and q < 0.05; a site is decreased when a peptide carrying it decreased and none increased: scripts/peptide_calls.py, as the CARM1 knockout of v1.3.1) and by the study's own rule. `call` follows the release's rule, except where the study's is the only sound call: the PRMT1 knockdown (2 against 2 samples) and the four Li et al. 2021 arms (one SILAC experiment each, no test). Peptide-level evidence with no total-protein correction. Site columns `perturbations`, `perturbation_calls`, `perturbation_calls_release_rule`, `perturbation_calls_source_rule`; one row per site and perturbation in `supporting/writer_perturbations`. The CARM1 knockout rows equal `supporting/carm1_ko`, which stays.

- CARM1 KO, MCF7 (Shishkova et al., Nat Commun 8:15571 (2017)): 363 sites quantified, 249 decreased and 2 increased by the release's rule; the two rules differ at 0 of the 363 sites both call.
- CARM1 KO, MDA-MB-231 (Shishkova et al., Nat Commun 8:15571 (2017)): 179 sites quantified, 111 decreased and 0 increased by the release's rule; the two rules differ at 0 of the 179 sites both call.
- PRMT7 KO, HCT116 (Szewczyk et al., Nat Commun 11:2396 (2020)): 333 sites quantified, 26 decreased and 3 increased by the release's rule; the two rules differ at 2 of the 333 sites both call.
- PRMT1 KD, HEK293T (Hartel et al., Mol Cell Proteomics 18:2149-2164 (2019)): 752 sites quantified, 60 decreased and 68 increased by the study's rule; the two rules differ at 124 of the 752 sites both call.
- PRMT5i EPZ015666, HeLa (Lu et al., J Proteome Res 23:1014-1027 (2024)): 338 sites quantified, 241 decreased and 1 increased by the release's rule; the two rules differ at 12 of the 335 sites both call.
- PRMT7 KD, HEK293 (Li WJ et al., Nat Commun 12:1946 (2021)): 795 sites quantified, 266 decreased and 0 increased by the study's rule; the release's rule cannot be applied.
- PRMT7i SGC3027, HEK293 (Li WJ et al., Nat Commun 12:1946 (2021)): 847 sites quantified, 455 decreased and 0 increased by the study's rule; the release's rule cannot be applied.
- CARM1 KD, HEK293 (Li WJ et al., Nat Commun 12:1946 (2021)): 962 sites quantified, 560 decreased and 0 increased by the study's rule; the release's rule cannot be applied.
- PRMT5 KD, HEK293 (Li WJ et al., Nat Commun 12:1946 (2021)): 385 sites listed, 385 decreased and 0 increased by the study's rule; the release's rule cannot be applied.

Source: Hartel et al., Mol Cell Proteomics 18:2149-2164 (2019), doi:10.1074/mcp.RA119.001625; Li WJ et al., Nat Commun 12:1946 (2021), doi:10.1038/s41467-021-21963-1; Lu et al., J Proteome Res 23:1014-1027 (2024), doi:10.1021/acs.jproteome.3c00724; Shishkova et al., Nat Commun 8:15571 (2017), doi:10.1038/ncomms15571; Szewczyk et al., Nat Commun 11:2396 (2020), doi:10.1038/s41467-020-16271-z. ### PaxDb 6.1 protein abundance

Protein abundance from PaxDb 6.1 (whole-organism integrated dataset) for each protein (`paxdb_abundance_ppm`, `paxdb_percentile`): a covariate for detection, since mass spectrometry finds abundant proteins most.

- 4,386 of 4,439 proteins have a value; their median percentile is 74.

Source: PaxDb 6.1, H. sapiens whole-organism integrated dataset (pax-db.org); Huang et al., Mol Cell Proteomics 22:100640 (2023), doi:10.1016/j.mcpro.2023.100640. Licence: CC BY 4.0.

### Reactome 97 pathways

Reactome release 97 pathways of each protein (`reactome_top_level`, `reactome_n_pathways`), and two over-representation tests of the methylated proteins against the reviewed proteins with a pathway (analyses `reactome_top_level`, `reactome_pathway`; clusterProfiler enricher, as the other protein sets).

- 20 of the 29 top-level pathways, and 597 of the 1,705 pathways of 10 to 500 proteins at any level, are enriched at q <= 0.05.

Source: Reactome release 97; Ragueneau et al., Nucleic Acids Res 54:D673-D681 (2026), doi:10.1093/nar/gkaf1223. Licence: CC0 (Reactome data; reactome.org/license).

### Curated functional experiments

Experiments from the literature on the methylation of a site or protein (`supporting/functional_experiments`), curated by hand from the papers (inputs/curation/README.md): what was done, what was measured and which way it changed. Kept apart from the site table and every statistic.

- 6 experiments so far, from 3 papers.

Source: the paper of each row. Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (this repository's data licence); the facts are from the papers cited.

- `supporting/source_records_v1.4.0.tsv`: one row per source record, with what the study reported, where each version placed it and the evidence check.

Their columns are listed in `README.md` and `data_dictionary.tsv`.
